## Notes

- The Android launcher icon is generated separately at `app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher.png`.
- Screenshots are not generated here. Capture them from a clean app session before uploading to Play Console.
- Google Play requires at least two screenshots across supported device types. Use real in-app screenshots, not mockups.
